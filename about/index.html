About me

Hello, I’m
Renefarious.

An independent software developer making practical tools for people who love to create.

Making complex things feel simpler.

My focus is Android development, especially tools that help people create and share Rusted Warfare mods.

With RW Mod Editor, I’m working on making mod creation more approachable on Android. I care about the small details: how an editor feels, how easily someone finds a property, and how many steps it takes to try an idea.

What keeps me building.

Questions, bug reports, and ideas help shape these projects. If you’ve used something I’ve made, I’d love to hear what worked and what could be better.
